About Me Research Publications Sci-Comm & Press

I am Gaia Rueda Moreno, a post-Baccalaureate urban ecologist, interested broadly in the relationships between pollinators and their plant partners. I received my B.A. from New York University, where I majored in Ecology and Environmental Studies. In the fall of 2022, I joined Dr. Kristin Winchell’s Urban Evolutionary Ecology lab, as an independent researcher. Applying what I learnt that summer, at an NSF-funded Research Experience for Undergraduates at Uconn & the Mystic Aquarium, I worked with my lab peers to develop projects investigating the recent invasion of the Spotted Lanternfly, as well as the intersections between ecology and socio-economics in New York City.

I am staunchly committed to inclusive science communication, in the face of growing public distrust in the scientific establishment. As an openly-transgender academic, I am keenly aware of how social and political issues intersect with research, and how academics often fail to advocate for the pressing issues at the center of their work. I strive to advocate for the marginalized communities impacted by environmental injustice, and to engage them in my current and future research.

Growing up in Northern Venezuela, my experience of nature was inseparable from the city. It was here that I grew my passion for discovering nature, as I hiked the barrancos outside of my home. In my spare time, I am an avid artist and photographer, particularly focused on wildlife. I also love to collect and preserve pinned insects, press flowers, and share my observations on iNaturalist.
