Life in The City Blog

Life in the City is a collaborative blog launched in 2018 by Kristin Winchell, Elizabeth Carlen, and Lindsay Miles. It is a hub for urban evolutionary ecologists to share and discuss their work, built by a diverse set of researchers at all levels of expertise. I am a frequent contributor to this blog, sharing my encounters with urban wildlife, while contextualizing urban ecology in the light of social and political struggles for justice.

the Bronx is Blooming

I began working with the Bronx is Blooming during the Summer of 2024, as a PLACE program Mentor. I led a cohort of 12 Bronx young adults in restoring the forests of Soundview Park, while engaging them in the environmental justice issues that face their community. Currently, I work as the organization’s Seasonal Tree Care Coordinator, maintaining our largest urban restoration project in Soundview Park, overseeing large volunteer projects, and pioneering citizen science initiatives. Using my experience with Geographic Information Systems and ecological research, I am helping the Bronx is Blooming to back up their efforts with quantitative results.
